## The problem

Down Syndrome Ireland needed more registrations for Ice Cream Funday. It wanted online interest to turn into money raised, and it needed to reach new audiences without spending much.

## What Andrew did

- Planned and ran an eight-week digital fundraising campaign
- Tested different ads and messages to see what people responded to
- Built a clear path from ad to registration to fundraising
- Used Enthuse to capture and manage leads

## Results

- €30,000+ raised online
- 554 registrations
- €6,000 ad spend
- €17.29 average cost per lead
