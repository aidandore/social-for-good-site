## The problem

The Irish Community Air Ambulance, now called CRITICAL, was small but busy and growing. It wanted Facebook Challenges to become a regular part of its fundraising.

## What Andrew did

- Ran the Meta advertising and helped set up a number of Facebook Challenges
- Supported several email campaigns
- Helped the charity choose web designers for its new website

## Results

The Facebook Challenges raised money and spread the word about the air ambulance's work.
