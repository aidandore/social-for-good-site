## The problem

Le Chéile needed more volunteers willing to mentor young people, and its website and social channels needed to say the same thing.

## What Andrew did

- Audited the website, social media and messaging
- Produced two short animations about the difference mentors make to young people
- Ran a Facebook lead generation campaign with several ad versions
- Worked with Fair City actor Neilí Conroy to get the campaign seen

## Results

The campaign reached people interested in volunteering, and Le Chéile got more of the right candidates.
